Cookie policy

This website does not currently set cookies, use analytics, show advertising or use tracking technologies.

Last updated: 4 August 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on a device by a website. They can be used for essential functions, preferences, audience measurement, advertising or tracking. Similar technologies include local storage, tracking pixels and device identifiers.

Cookies used by this website

None. Simpson Singing School does not currently place first-party or third-party cookies on your device. It does not use analytics, advertising pixels, social media plugins or embedded third-party media.

Because no non-essential cookies are used, a cookie consent banner is not required or displayed.

The enquiry form

The enquiry form prepares an email in your own email application. The website does not send the form to a website database and does not use cookies or browser storage to retain the form contents.

If you choose “Copy enquiry”, your browser’s clipboard feature is used only at that moment to copy the message you prepared.

Hosting logs

Like most websites, the hosting provider may automatically process technical request information such as an IP address, browser type, requested page, date and time for security and service operation. These server logs are not cookies and are controlled by the hosting provider.

See the Privacy Notice for more information about how personal information is handled.

Browser controls

You can block or delete cookies using your browser settings. As this website does not currently set cookies, changing those settings should not affect its normal operation.

Future changes

If analytics, embedded services or other technologies are introduced, this policy will be updated first. Where the law requires consent, those technologies will not be activated until the visitor has made a choice.
